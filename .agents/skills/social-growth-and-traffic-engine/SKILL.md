## 📱 1. Platform Algorithm Decryption

### A. Instagram Algorithm (Reels & Carousels)
- **Primary Signals**: Watch time completion rate, Save-to-Share ratio, 3-second visual hook.
- **Reel Strategy**:
  - *Hook (0–3s)*: High-contrast video opening (e.g. desert fortress sunrise or Himalayan pass drop) with text overlay: *"India is not a destination. It is a route — surveyed, sealed, assigned."*
  - *Audio*: Use trending atmospheric/cinematic ambient audio tracks under 30 seconds.
  - *Caption*: First 2 lines contain primary curiosity gap; include targeted travel hashtags (`#LuxuryTravelIndia`, `#Rajputana`, `#Ladakh27N`, `#UnlistedIndia`).
- **Carousel Strategy**:
  - Slide 1: High-impact image + bold headline (*"5 Places in India Not Advertised on Open Markets"*).
  - Slide 2–6: 6-Pass audit breakdown, 5-hour road limit, Protected Area Permits.
  - Slide 7 (CTA): *"Link in Bio to request an unsealed executive dossier."*

### B. X / Twitter Algorithm
- **Primary Signals**: Bookmark velocity, Reply depth, Quote-retweets, Media attachments.
- **Thread Architecture**:
  - *Tweet 1 (Hook)*: *"Most travelers in India experience departure fatigue. Here is how we coordinate routes that peak in the final 48 hours:"*
  - *Tweet 2–5*: Case studies of the 4 Corridors (Rajputana, Ladakh, Kerala, Sikkim).
  - *Tweet 6 (CTA)*: Insert website link in the **reply** or final tweet to bypass main tweet link algorithmic penalty.
- **Bookmark Optimization**: Include actionable checklists (*"The 6-Pass Audit Checklist for UHNW Travel in India"*).

### C. Facebook Algorithm
- **Primary Signals**: Long-form video watch time (1min+), Meaningful social interactions (comments/shares), High-trust Group posts.
- **Group & Page Strategy**:
  - Share long-form executive travel notes in luxury travel & concierge communities.
  - Post native high-resolution video reels with custom captions and link callouts.
